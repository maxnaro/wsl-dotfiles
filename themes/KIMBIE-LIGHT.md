# Kimbie Light attribution

The light palette in `chocolatier.json` and generated Kimbie themes are adapted
from **Kimbie Light** by **Jan T. Sott (idleberg)**:

- Source: https://github.com/idleberg/atom-kimbie-light
- Palette: https://github.com/idleberg/atom-kimbie-light/blob/master/styles/colors.less
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
  (CC BY-SA 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

These adaptations retain the cream background and brown foreground, map colors
to terminal/UI roles, and darken accents and secondary labels for readable text
at 75% terminal opacity. The adapted palette and generated Kimbie theme assets
are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. Chocolatier dark is unchanged.
